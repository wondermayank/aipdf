FAQ

Good to know

Is ThunderDoc free to use?

Yes. ThunderDoc is free to use. Describe what you need and generate a downloadable document with no sign-up required.

What file formats can I download?

You can generate and download Word (.docx), PDF, PowerPoint (.pptx) and Excel (.xlsx) files directly in your browser.

Do I need to sign up to use ThunderDoc?

No account is required. Type your prompt, choose a format, and download your file immediately.

Can students use ThunderDoc for assignments and study notes?

Yes. ThunderDoc works well for essays, assignments, study notes, lesson plans and exam revision material, in addition to resumes, reports and business documents.

Is my prompt or content stored anywhere?

Your prompt is sent to the AI model only to generate your document and is not stored or used for anything else.
